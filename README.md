# Himalaya-Climate-Study# Climate-Driven Landscape Dynamics in the Central Himalayas (1990–2026)

## 🔬 Project Overview
This project utilizes 36 years of Landsat satellite imagery (Landsat 5, 7, 8, and 9) to analyze the impacts of climate warming on cryosphere degradation, vegetation growth, and land surface temperature in the Central Himalayas. 

## 💡 Novel Innovation: The Cryosphere-Vegetation Vulnerability Index (CVVI)
Traditional studies only look at raw temperature changes. This project introduces the **CVVI**, a new model that mathematically links atmospheric warming, ice loss (NDSI), and vegetation greening (NDVI) to identify "tipping points" in high-altitude landscapes.

### 📊 Key Statistical Findings (Winter Season)
- **Air Temp vs. Snow/Ice (NDSI):** `-0.61` (Strong negative correlation: as temperature rises, ice disappears).
- **Air Temp vs. Land Surface Temp (LST):** `+0.64` (Surface warming is tightly coupled with atmospheric warming).
- **Air Temp vs. Vegetation (NDVI):** `+0.37` (Warmer winters are triggering high-altitude vegetation greening).
